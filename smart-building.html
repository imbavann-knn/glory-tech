Solution Areas

What We Deliver

Four integrated capabilities that together transform how a building operates.

01 — Management
Smart Building Management System
An AI-powered platform that unifies every building system into a single pane of glass. Real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, and automated response — across security, environment, energy, and facilities.
  • Intelligent security: AI facial recognition, intrusion detection, RFID access control
  • Environmental control: occupancy-based lighting, HVAC, air quality (PM2.5, CO₂)
  • Energy management: smart metering, renewable integration, anomaly detection
  • Facility operations: meeting room booking, parking management, work order tracking
02 — Conference
Smart Conference Systems
End-to-end audio-visual conference solutions that enable seamless in-room and hybrid collaboration. From boardrooms to auditoriums — engineered for clarity, reliability, and ease of use.
  • Professional display systems: interactive panels, projection, video walls
  • Audio: ceiling microphones, speaker arrays, noise cancellation
  • One-touch meeting start with room booking integration
  • Works with Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and other UC platforms
03 — Display
LED Display Systems
High-impact LED display solutions for lobbies, retail concourses, command centres, and outdoor façades. Scalable from small indoor panels to large-format curved and fine-pitch displays.
  • Fine-pitch indoor LED: P1.2 to P2.5, suitable for control rooms and broadcast
  • Outdoor waterproof LED: P3 to P10, high-brightness façade and signage
  • Transparent and creative-form LED panels for architectural integration
  • Centralised content management and scheduling software
04 — Campus
Smart Campus & Video Collaboration
Integrated campus-wide management combining security, communications, and facility systems under one unified platform. Extend collaboration beyond meeting rooms with enterprise-grade video conferencing across sites.
  • Multi-building unified access control and visitor management
  • Intercampus HD video conferencing with low-latency codec
  • Campus-wide announcement and PA integration
  • GIS-based 3D building map for operations teams
